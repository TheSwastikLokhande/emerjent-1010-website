# PC & Laptop Repair Before/After Photos

Dump all your repair photos in this directory!

## Recommended Naming & Folder Structure

You can organize your photos in two simple ways:

### Option 1: Direct File Naming in `public/repairs/` (Recommended)

Save your image files directly in `public/repairs/` using these standard names:

#### Maintenance & Cleaning Services:
- **Internal Deep Cleaning**: `cleaning-before.jpg` & `cleaning-after.jpg`
- **Thermal Repasting**: `thermal-before.jpg` & `thermal-after.jpg`
- **Performance Optimization**: `performance-before.jpg` & `performance-after.jpg`
- **Preventive Maintenance**: `preventive-before.jpg` & `preventive-after.jpg`
- **System Diagnostics**: `diagnostics-before.jpg` & `diagnostics-after.jpg`

#### Fabrication & Hardware Repair Services:
- **Hinge Repair**: `hinge-repair-before.jpg` & `hinge-repair-after.jpg`
- **Hinge Fabrication**: `hinge-fab-before.jpg` & `hinge-fab-after.jpg`
- **Laptop Body Repair**: `body-repair-before.jpg` & `body-repair-after.jpg`
- **Plastic Welding**: `plastic-welding-before.jpg` & `plastic-welding-after.jpg`
- **Cosmetic Restoration**: `cosmetic-before.jpg` & `cosmetic-after.jpg`
- **Precision Repairs**: `precision-before.jpg` & `precision-after.jpg`

---

### Option 2: Using Subfolders (`public/repairs/before/` and `public/repairs/after/`)

If you prefer keeping Before and After photos in separate folders:
- Drop "Before" photos in: `public/repairs/before/`
- Drop "After" photos in: `public/repairs/after/`

Accepted formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`
